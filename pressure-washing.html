Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pressure washing cost?

Your estimate depends on the surface material, area, condition and access. Send your address and photos for a personal quote before we begin.

Do you use high pressure on every surface?

No. We choose the method for the material and its condition. Some surfaces need a gentler approach, and we confirm what is suitable before cleaning.

Can you remove every stain?

Results depend on the stain and surface. Deep oil marks, rust, old coatings and permanent wear may remain or need separate treatment. Photos help us set realistic expectations.

Does deck or fence washing include staining?

Washing does not automatically include sanding or staining. If you want deck or fence refinishing, mention it in your request so we can quote the full scope separately.

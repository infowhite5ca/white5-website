Helpful answers

Deck Cleaning and Staining FAQ

Do you clean decks without staining them?

Yes. Deck cleaning can be quoted separately when the surface is suitable and staining is not part of the current project.

How long does a deck need to dry before staining?

Drying time depends on weather, shade, wood and the preparation method. We schedule stain application only when conditions are appropriate.

Can you stain over an existing finish?

Sometimes, but compatibility and condition matter. Peeling, glossy or failing coatings may need additional preparation before a new finish can bond properly.

How long will the new stain last?

Longevity depends on product, preparation, sun exposure, moisture, traffic and maintenance. Horizontal surfaces typically weather faster than vertical areas.

How do you price deck cleaning and staining?

Pricing considers square footage, railings, stairs, current coating, required preparation, access and stain system. Photos are strongly recommended for the estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does window cleaning cost?

Your estimate depends on the number and size of windows, access, and services you choose. Send us your property address and a few details for a personal quote.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not for exterior-only cleaning, as long as we have agreed on safe access. For interior cleaning, someone needs to be home unless we arrange otherwise.

Can you clean screens and tracks?

Yes. Screen cleaning and accessible track cleaning are available. Mention them in your request so we can include them in your estimate.

Will purified water remove hard-water stains?

Purified water cleaning removes everyday dirt and residue. Established mineral stains, paint, or damaged glass may need separate treatment. Send a photo so we can advise before the job.

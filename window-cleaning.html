Helpful answers

Window Cleaning FAQ

How much does window cleaning cost in Calgary?

Pricing depends on window quantity and size, storeys, access, screens and the level of buildup. White5 provides a free estimate before scheduling.

Do you clean exterior and interior windows?

Exterior window cleaning is available, and interior glass, screens or tracks can be included when requested. Confirm the scope in your estimate.

Do I need to be home?

For exterior-only work, you may not need to be home if we have safe access and the details are confirmed in advance.

Can you clean second-storey windows?

Yes, subject to safe access and site conditions. Photos help us plan the correct equipment before arrival.

How often should windows be cleaned?

Many Calgary homeowners schedule once or twice per year, commonly in spring and fall. Exposure, trees and nearby construction can change the ideal frequency.

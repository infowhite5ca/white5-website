Frequently Asked Questions

How much does deck or fence work cost?

Your estimate depends on the wood condition, size, existing coating, preparation, access and the work you choose. Send photos and your address for a personal quote.

What is included in deck cleaning and staining?

The scope can include cleaning, surface preparation, sanding and stain application. We confirm exactly what is included before work begins.

Do you supply the stain?

You choose and normally supply the stain. We confirm the product and colour before beginning any staining work.

Can you repair damaged deck boards?

Minor repairs may be possible. Major structural concerns, rot or unsafe railings need separate assessment and are not assumed to be part of standard cleaning or staining.

Helpful answers

Gutter Cleaning FAQ

How often should gutters be cleaned in Calgary?

Most homes benefit from cleaning at least once a year. Homes near mature trees may need spring and fall service.

Do you clean downspouts?

Accessible downspout openings are checked during service. Severe underground or enclosed blockages may require a separate solution.

Can you clean gutters on a two-storey home?

Yes, subject to safe access, ground conditions and roofline configuration. Please mention height when requesting an estimate.

What happens to the debris?

Removed material is collected and the immediate work area is left tidy.

Why is gutter cleaning important?

Blocked gutters can overflow near siding, foundations and walkways. Routine cleaning supports proper drainage and helps identify visible issues early.
